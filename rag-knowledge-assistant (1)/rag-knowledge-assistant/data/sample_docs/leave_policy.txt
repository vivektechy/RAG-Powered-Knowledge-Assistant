LEAVE POLICY

1. Annual Leave
Every full-time employee is entitled to 18 paid annual leave days per calendar year.
Leave accrues at a rate of 1.5 days per completed month of service. Unused annual
leave can be carried forward to the next year up to a maximum of 10 days. Any
balance beyond 10 days will lapse on December 31st.

2. Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 10 paid sick leave days per year. A medical certificate
is required for sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days. Sick leave does not carry
forward to the next year.

3. Maternity and Paternity Leave
Maternity leave is 26 weeks of paid leave for the primary caregiver. Paternity
leave is 2 weeks of paid leave, to be taken within 3 months of the child's birth.

4. Leave Application Process
All leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 3 working
days in advance, except in emergencies. Leave requests are approved by the
employee's direct manager. Approval typically takes 1-2 business days.

5. Unpaid Leave
Employees who have exhausted their paid leave balance may request unpaid leave,
subject to manager and HR approval. Unpaid leave beyond 30 days requires
Director-level sign-off.

6. Public Holidays
The company observes 12 public holidays per year, as published in the HR
calendar at the start of each fiscal year.
