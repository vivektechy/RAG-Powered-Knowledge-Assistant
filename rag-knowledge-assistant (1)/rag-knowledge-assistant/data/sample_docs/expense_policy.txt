EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY

1. Eligible Expenses
Business travel (flights, trains, taxis), client meals, accommodation for
business trips, and approved conference/training fees are reimbursable.
Personal expenses, alcohol (unless part of an approved client entertainment
budget), and first-class travel upgrades are not reimbursable.

2. Submission Process
All expense claims must be submitted through the Expense Portal within 30
days of the expense being incurred. Claims must include an itemized receipt
for any single expense over 500 rupees. Claims without receipts above this
threshold will be rejected.

3. Approval Limits
Expenses under 5,000 rupees require only direct manager approval.
Expenses between 5,000 and 25,000 rupees require manager plus department
head approval. Expenses above 25,000 rupees require Finance team pre-approval
before the expense is incurred.

4. Travel Booking
All flights and hotels must be booked through the approved corporate travel
portal. Economy class is standard for flights under 5 hours; business class
may be approved for flights over 5 hours with Director sign-off.

5. Reimbursement Timeline
Approved claims are reimbursed within the next payroll cycle, typically
within 15 business days of approval.

6. Per Diem
For domestic business travel, employees receive a per diem of 1,500 rupees
per day to cover meals and incidentals, in lieu of itemized meal receipts.
