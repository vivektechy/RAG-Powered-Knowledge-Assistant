IT SUPPORT FAQ

Q: How do I reset my company password?
A: Go to portal.company.com/reset, enter your employee ID, and follow the
email verification steps. Passwords must be at least 12 characters and
include one number and one special character. Password resets are also
available by calling the IT helpdesk at extension 4040.

Q: How do I get a new laptop?
A: Submit a hardware request through the IT Service Desk portal under
"Asset Requests." Standard laptop requests are fulfilled within 5 business
days. Urgent requests (device failure, new hire starting within 48 hours)
should be flagged as "Priority" and are typically fulfilled within 1 business day.

Q: How do I connect to the office VPN?
A: Install the GlobalProtect VPN client from the Software Center, then log
in with your standard company credentials. VPN access must first be
approved by your manager through the Access Request form.

Q: Who do I contact for software installation requests?
A: Standard approved software (listed in the Software Catalog) can be
self-installed via the Software Center. Non-standard software requires
a request through IT with manager approval, and is reviewed by the
Security team before installation.

Q: What do I do if I suspect a phishing email?
A: Do not click any links or download attachments. Use the "Report Phishing"
button in Outlook, which forwards the email directly to the Security team.
Do not forward suspicious emails manually.

Q: How long does the IT helpdesk take to respond?
A: Standard tickets: within 1 business day. High priority tickets (system
down, security incident): within 1 hour. The helpdesk is available
Monday-Friday, 9 AM - 6 PM.
